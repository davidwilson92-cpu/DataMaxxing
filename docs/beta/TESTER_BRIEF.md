# Zova Social — private beta

Zova helps turn your ideas into posts in your own voice. This beta is free: no card
or subscription payment is needed. Use content you are comfortable testing with.
Tell us when something is unclear as well as when something breaks.

## Your first session

1. Install the invited build through TestFlight and create your own Zova account.
   Check that the name and email shown belong to you.
2. In Studio, enter an idea and review the AI-sharing choice. Create a caption,
   edit it, add an image or short video, then close and reopen the app.
   Your saved conversation, caption and attachment should still be there.
3. Open Next move. If you have no plan, use **Set your goal**. To edit an existing
   plan, use Studio's **Post options → Your plan & recurring drafts**. Choose one
   suggested idea and turn it into a draft. Tell us whether it was useful.
4. Connect only a social account you own. Check the displayed destination before
   confirming the connection. Try cancelling once and then connecting successfully.
   The iPhone journey should return to Zova.
5. Review a post without publishing it. Verify the caption, attachment, account,
   platform options and time zone. Nothing should be sent before you confirm.
6. Try Drafts, Performance and Account. Check that missing analytics are explained
   and that settings remain saved after reopening.

Publishing is optional. If you choose to publish, use a harmless test post on an
account you own. Check both Zova's result and the destination. If the result says
unconfirmed, **do not resend**; send us the support reference.

## Please try these edge cases

- Switch away and back while writing; close and reopen after saving.
- Lose your connection briefly, attempt a save, then reconnect. Your input should
  remain available and the save state should be honest.
- Change a caption after reviewing it. Zova should require another review.
- Schedule, reschedule and cancel your own test draft; verify your time zone.
- Sign out and sign back in. Another account must never see your drafts or connections.
- Try larger text, VoiceOver and landscape if you use them normally.
- Request a password-reset email once the beta invitation confirms recovery is live.

## Current platform limits

Instagram requires a Business or Creator profile. Facebook publishes to Pages,
not personal timelines. X depends on the account's API access and limits.
TikTok currently sends to your inbox for completion in TikTok; direct feed posting
is not part of this external beta until the production permission review is complete.

## Send useful feedback

Use TestFlight's **Send Beta Feedback**, or email
[support@zova-social.com](mailto:support@zova-social.com) through Zova's **Help**.
Include:

- What you were trying to do.
- The steps you took, what you expected and what happened.
- App build, iPhone model/iOS version, or browser.
- A screenshot or short recording and any support reference.
- Whether the issue prevented you finishing, and whether it happened again.

Never send passwords, verification codes, social access tokens or private messages.
Hide other people's personal information in screenshots.

At the end, tell us: **Would you use Zova again next week? What is the one thing
that would make it more useful?**
