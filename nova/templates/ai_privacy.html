Create with Zova’s AI?

When you use AI, Zova sends your relevant ideas, drafts, saved voice and strategy to OpenAI. Attached images and sampled video frames can be included. Audio is not analysed.

Performance questions can include recent post excerpts and available metrics. Next move can use your public content themes in web searches.

You can turn this off in Account → Security & privacy. This stops new AI requests; it does not recall information already sent. Saved work stays editable. Privacy details
