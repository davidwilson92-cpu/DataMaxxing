Delete connected data

You stay in control of the accounts and information connected to Zova.

Disconnect a social account

Sign in to Zova, open Account, and choose Unlink beside the relevant platform. Zova removes the stored authorization for that account and can no longer read from or publish to it.

Delete your Zova account

Open Account → Delete account in Zova and confirm with your password. This signs you out everywhere and cancels unsent schedules across your brands. Keep your receipt to see when active account data has been erased.

Erasure is processed after any sends already in progress, active subscriptions and required retention are checked. Published social posts are not deleted. If you cannot sign in, email {{ contact_email }} from the address registered to your account for help.

Remove access at the platform

You can also remove Zova from the connected-app or business-integration settings inside Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X or Apple.
