Your first post with Zova

  1. Start in Studio: select platforms and describe your idea, audience and desired outcome. You can draft before connecting an account.
  2. Attach your own images or video if needed. When you send a message, Zova sends attached images or sampled video frames to OpenAI for visual inspection. It does not listen to audio or fetch source links. Check its observations and correct any mistakes. For Instagram, choose Post or Story beside the platform selection; a Story publishes your media without adding caption text or stickers. Automatic Stories require a Business account.
  3. Check and refine each version. Wait for “Saved”; if a save fails, use Retry save or download your work. Find it later in Drafts.
  4. When ready to publish, connect your destination in Socials. Instagram supports direct login or Facebook login for a linked professional account.
  5. Select Publish or Schedule. Review the exact account, text, media, time and settings before confirming. Pending is not published.

Editing and recovery

Change your voice preferences whenever they are inaccurate. If a save fails, keep the tab open: your input stays visible. “Open saved version” reloads the server copy and discards unsaved local changes only after the browser warning. Keep a copy before replacing them.

Scheduled posts and uncertain results

Manage future jobs in Drafts. Cancellation and rescheduling stop once a job starts. Enter a schedule in your saved account timezone; check the timezone and exact time in the final review. Draft-list times may use your browser timezone. If a provider response is uncertain, check the destination before taking further action; Zova will not automatically resend it. Only definitely failed platforms can be retried.
