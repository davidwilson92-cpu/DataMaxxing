From idea to post

Describe your idea. Zova drafts the caption. You edit, review and choose when it goes live.

  1. Create in Studio. Choose your platforms and add an idea or media. Connecting an account can wait until publishing.
  2. Make it yours. Edit the caption or ask Zova to refine it. Wait for “Saved”.
  3. Review, then share. Choose Publish or Schedule and confirm the account, content and time.
Next Move and your strategy

Next Move suggests one useful action. Draft it, change it or move to another idea. To plan further ahead, open Your strategy, describe your goal and save Zova’s proposed plan. You can edit it later in Account.

Current-topic ideas cite public sources; they do not inspect private feeds or prove a topic is trending. Evergreen ideas are based on your strategy.

Schedule and saved drafts

Schedule shows your calendar and saved posts. Select a scheduled post to change its time, edit its content or cancel. Editing cancels its pending deliveries so you can review the new version before scheduling it again.

Schedule shows times in your device timezone; the final review also identifies your account timezone. Once delivery starts, it cannot be cancelled or edited. An unconfirmed outcome needs a destination check; Zova will not automatically resend it.

Connecting social accounts

Your Zova account and social accounts have separate logins. Check the profile Zova shows before confirming.

Instagram needs a public Creator or Business account; automatic Stories need a Business account. Facebook connects a Page you manage, not a personal profile. If Facebook asks for Google sign-in, complete that account’s usual sign-in on Facebook, then return to Zova and try again. Zova cannot change Facebook’s login method.

Images, video and music

With your permission, AI checks images and sampled video frames. It does not listen to audio. Review the proposed caption.

Choose Instagram Post or Story below the platform buttons. Story planning notes are not overlaid on the media. To add Instagram’s music or stickers to a Story or photo, save your media and finish in Instagram. Zova does not mark that handoff as published.

Your voice and privacy

Your voice saves the writing guidance Zova uses. You can edit it, learn from recent posts or clear learned examples. Your edits take priority.

Account contains AI-sharing and security controls, plus Delete account. Turning off AI sharing stops new AI requests; saved work remains editable.

Sign-in and saving problems

Reset your password if you cannot sign in. Check Spam for the email.

If saving fails, keep Studio open and use Retry save or Download your work. Opening another saved version requires resolving unsaved changes first.
