From idea to post

  1. Bring an idea. Choose your platforms in Studio and describe what you want to say. You can draft before connecting accounts.
  2. Make it yours. Add your media, edit the caption directly or ask Zova to refine it. Wait for “Saved”.
  3. Review and send. Choose Publish or Schedule, check the exact account, content and settings, then confirm.
  4. Come back to your work. Drafts holds saved posts, schedules and delivery results. Performance helps you choose what to try next.
Ideas, strategy and recurring drafts

Next move shows one suggestion at a time. Use the arrows to see another, Change this idea to give feedback, or Draft post to create it. Opening an existing suggestion returns to its saved draft.

In Studio, open Post options → Your plan & recurring drafts to add or edit your strategy and plan a series. Fresh drafts need individual approval. Repeated posts need a review of the exact content, destinations and dates. Reminding yourself about an idea does not schedule a post.

Current-topic suggestions use public web sources checked against your confirmed strategy. They do not inspect private feeds or prove that a topic is trending. Evergreen ideas do not need a current source. Check linked sources before using their claims.

Images, video and Instagram Stories

With your permission, AI inspects resized images and sampled video frames when you send a message. It does not listen to audio or read the contents of your source link. Check its observations.

When Instagram is selected, choose Post or Story below the platform buttons. A Story publishes your media without adding caption text or stickers. Automatic Stories require a Business account; other Instagram publishing needs a Creator or Business account. Both Instagram login routes remain available.

Saves, errors and delivery status

If saving fails, keep the page open and use Retry save or download your work. Opening a saved version replaces unsaved local changes only after the warning.

Pending does not mean published. An unconfirmed outcome needs a destination check; Zova will not automatically resend it. Only definitely failed platforms can be retried.

Manage future posts in the weekly agenda in Drafts. Scheduling uses your saved account timezone; review the exact time before confirming. Cancellation and rescheduling stop once delivery starts. Pausing a recurring series cancels pending deliveries; resuming alone does not restart them.

Voice, accounts and AI sharing

In Account → Your voice, edit the guidance used for drafts. Scanning recent posts is optional. Your Zova login and your social logins are separate; check the selected profile before confirming a connection.

Security & privacy lets you change your password, review AI sharing and request deletion. Turning off AI sharing stops new AI requests; saved work stays editable. Password recovery needs configured email delivery.
