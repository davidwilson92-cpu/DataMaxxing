Your first post with Zova

  1. Connect your destination in Socials. Instagram offers direct Instagram login and Facebook login for a linked professional account.
  2. In Studio, select platforms and describe your idea, audience and desired outcome. You can attach your own images or video.
  3. Check each version. Zova drafts text; it does not inspect attached media or fetch source links. Describe their contents so it can work from accurate information.
  4. Wait for “Saved” or select Save now. Open the draft later from Drafts.
  5. Select Publish or Schedule. Review the exact destination, text, media and platform settings before confirming. A pending result is not a published post.

Editing and recovery

Change your voice preferences whenever they are inaccurate. If a save fails, keep the tab open: your input stays visible. “Open saved version” reloads the server copy and discards unsaved local changes only after the browser warning. Keep a copy before replacing them.

Scheduled posts and uncertain results

Manage future jobs in Drafts. Cancellation and rescheduling stop once a job starts. Times are shown in your browser’s timezone. If a provider response is uncertain, check the destination before taking further action; Zova will not automatically resend it. Only definitely failed platforms can be retried.

Current testing offer

Subscription enforcement is disabled for testing. This workspace does not require a paid plan. Platform permissions and approvals still determine which connections can publish. Connecting an account does not guarantee every feature is approved.

Account access

Change your password in Account. Other sessions are revoked when a password changes. Password recovery is available from the login page when secure email delivery is configured.
